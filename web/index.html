A budget that knows where every coin is going.

Embers is an envelope budget: you plan the money you already have, category by category, before you spend it. Every account can hold its own currency, your bank's foreign-exchange fee is counted for you, and everything stays in your own iCloud.

Features

Everything a careful budget needs, nothing it doesn't.

Built for people who want to see exactly where their money stands — in more than one country, on more than one device.

Envelope budgeting, done properly

Give the money you have a purpose before you spend it. Assign to bills, everyday spending and long-term goals; see at a glance what is left in each, and move money between categories when life changes. Plan months ahead, and look back at what any past month had left.

Accounts in any currency

Hold a card in euros, a salary account in dollars and cash in pounds. Type an amount in whatever you paid and Embers converts it — adding your bank's foreign-transaction fee when the currency isn't the account's own.

Credit cards that add up

Spend on a card from a funded category and the money moves to the card's payment, so you always know what you owe and whether it's covered. A card in credit balances the books too.

Transfers, on and off budget

Move money between accounts in one step, even across currencies. Track investments, property or loans alongside your budget, and count money moving into them against the right category.

Scheduled and repeating

Set up rent, salaries and subscriptions once. A scheduled payment waits for your approval before it counts, so a subscription you cancelled is never spent from your plan by accident.

Reminders that come from your device

A nudge when a payment is due — approve or skip it right from the notification — plus warnings when a category won't cover an upcoming bill, before a card payment, and at the start and end of each month.

Reports worth reading

Where your money went, what came in against what went out, and your net worth over time — with a plain-English verdict, and colours chosen to stay readable for colour-blind eyes.

Reconcile with your bank

Mark entries as cleared, compare against your statement, and lock in a reconciled balance. Embers reminds you when an account hasn't been checked in a while, and a budget check points out anything that looks wrong.

Quick to enter

A big keypad, the account's currency picked for you, and your payees remembered. On the Mac, type a pile of receipts in a row with the keyboard alone.

Native on iPhone, iPad and Mac

The iPhone app is made for one hand. The Mac app is a real Mac app, not a phone app in a window: a sidebar with every account and its balance, sortable tables, amounts typed straight into the plan, an inspector beside it, and keyboard shortcuts throughout. Light and dark appearance on both.

Money across borders

Pay in euros. Budget in dollars. See the real cost.

Banks don't just convert — they add a fee, and bill it as one line. Embers records the purchase the way your statement will show it.

  • Every account remembers its own currency; entries default to it.
  • Daily rates for 160+ currencies, kept on the device so entry works offline.
  • A default fee for your plan, or a different one for each card.
  • Type the bank's actual rate when it differed — the original amount is kept, so every conversion can be checked months later.

Your data

Yours, on every device. Never ours.

There is no Embers account and no Embers server. Your budget moves between your devices through your own private iCloud.

iCloud sync

Enter a purchase on your iPhone and it's on your Mac moments later. Two devices editing at once don't undo each other, and a new device shows its budget arriving rather than an empty one.

Backup and restore

Save a complete backup to iCloud Drive whenever you like, in an open, readable format you can see in the Files app. Restore it on any device — and Embers reminds you when it's been a while.

Import from YNAB

Already budgeting? Bring your history with you: import a YNAB export and your accounts, categories, assignments, transfers, splits and card payments come across, with each account's currency recognised from its name.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Embers uses the Apple ID you're already signed in with to sync through your own iCloud. There's nothing to sign up for, and no password of ours to remember.

Where is my budget stored? Can you see it?

On your devices, and in your private iCloud database when sync is on. The developer has no server and no way to see your budget. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Does Embers connect to my bank?

No. You enter transactions yourself — which is what keeps you aware of every purchase — and reconcile against your statement when you like. The only thing Embers fetches from the internet is the day's exchange rates.

If it's open source, why does it cost money?

Publishing on the App Store has real costs, starting with Apple's yearly developer fee. The price covers those. If you'd rather, you're free to build Embers yourself from the source code.

Can I bring my existing budget?

Yes. Embers imports a YNAB export, including past months, transfers, split transactions and credit cards. You can also restore any backup made by Embers itself.

Does one purchase cover iPhone, iPad and Mac?

Embers runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac, and syncs between them through iCloud. Purchase details will be confirmed here when the App Store listing goes live.
